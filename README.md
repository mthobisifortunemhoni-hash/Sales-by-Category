# Sales-by-Category

# Power BI - Superstore Sales Analysis (Sales by Category)

## Objectives

Analyze sales performance across product categories using Microsoft Power BI and identify business insights from the Superstore dataset to support business decision-making and improve sales performance.


## Visualization

![Sales-by-Category](https://github.com/mthobisifortunemhoni-hash/Sales-by-Category/blob/730388f0f16de009de05c7bf664b30e0c09280cf/Sales-by-Category.png)


## Business Question

Which product category generates the highest sales?


## Findings

- Technology generated the highest total sales.
- Office Supplies generated the second-highest total sales.
- Furniture generated the lowest total sales.
  

## Business Insights

- Technology is the strongest-performing category and contributes the largest share of sales revenue.
- Office Supplies also performs well and represents a significant portion of total sales.
- Furniture has the lowest sales among the three categories and may require further investigation.

## Recommendations

- Continue investing in the Technology category to maintain its strong performance.
- Maintain the performance of Office Supplies through effective inventory management and marketing.
- Investigate the Furniture category to determine factors affecting sales, such as pricing, promotions, customer preferences, or product availability.
